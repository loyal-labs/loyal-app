Money has two jobs. It needs to be ready when a customer needs it, and it should earn when they do not.

Loyal handles both. The customer chooses how much USDC stays available. Loyal checks the balance and moves only the eligible amount into an approved Earn position. The customer keeps root control of the account.

<Info>
  **Available today:** Balance Sweep & Earn. Yield is variable, and supplied
  funds remain exposed to the risks of the underlying lending market.
</Info>

## What that means for a customer

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="Keep money ready" icon="wallet">
    The customer sets the USDC amount they want to keep available for spending, refunds, or payouts.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Earn on the rest" icon="chart-line">
    USDC above that reserve can move into the customer's Earn position through an approved USDC path.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stay in control" icon="shield-halved">
    The customer approves access and can pause new sweeps, withdraw, or approve closure.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Loyal does the recurring work. It does not receive the customer's private key, grant itself root authority, or choose an unrestricted destination.

## Why automate this

Without automation, someone has to notice each new balance, decide what must remain available, and move the rest by hand. That delay leaves usable capital untouched and creates more work as a product grows.

In a mid-August 2025 snapshot, Gauntlet identified **$1.05 billion, or 11.8%, of Solana USDC in DeFi protocols**. Its methodology covered direct USDC balances associated with the top 100 protocols by total value locked. [Read the report](https://www.gauntlet.xyz/resources/thaw-potential-how-defi-can-unlock-value-for-46-billion-in-usdc).

<Note>
  Money outside those measured positions is not automatically idle or safe to
  deploy. Customers still need operating liquidity and reserves.
</Note>

## Start with one bounded job

Balance Sweep & Earn is Loyal's first bounded job. The same model can support other financial workflows when each one has a clear trigger, an allowed action, a limit, verifiable evidence, and a customer-controlled exit.

That possibility does not mean every example is available today. The [Automation catalog](/automations/catalog) separates the live product from illustrative and unsupported workflows.
