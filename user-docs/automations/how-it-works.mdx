Imagine 10,000 USDC arrives in a customer's account. They want 2,000 USDC ready for everyday use, but nobody should have to notice and move the other 8,000 USDC by hand.

That surplus can go to work.

The customer sets that reserve and approves one narrow path to Earn. Loyal can then do the recurring work only within that approval.

<Info>
  **Live today:** USDC Balance Sweep and Earn. Rates are variable, and funds are
  exposed to the risks of the underlying lending market.
</Info>

<AutomationWalkthrough />

The reserve guides the amount Loyal tries to keep available. Separate onchain controls limit how much may move and where it may go.

Next, see how Loyal [calculates the eligible amount](/automations/reserve-and-eligibility) and [routes it into Earn](/automations/routing-and-yield). The [Exit and lifecycle](/automations/exit-and-lifecycle) page explains what each way of stopping changes.
