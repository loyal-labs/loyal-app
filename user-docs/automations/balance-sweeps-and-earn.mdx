---
title: "Balance Sweep & Earn"
description: "What Loyal's live automation does, what the customer chooses, and where its boundary ends."
---

A customer may need USDC ready for spending, refunds, or payouts. Keeping every dollar in the Main Account is simple, but it leaves the surplus untouched. Moving that surplus by hand creates work and delay.

Balance Sweep & Earn gives it a default job.

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| What stays ready? | The USDC reserve chosen by the customer. |
| What can move? | Eligible USDC above that reserve, further limited by the approved recurring amount. |
| Where does it go? | The customer's Earn position through an approved USDC lending path. |
| Who controls access? | The customer approves setup and can pause, withdraw, or approve closure. |

<Warning>
  The reserve guides Loyal's scheduler. It cannot guarantee that future
  payments will clear. Loyal does not predict a card payment, refund, or payout
  and withdraw from Earn before it occurs.
</Warning>
