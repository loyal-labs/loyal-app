Loyal can support revenue beyond interchange when a business has a contractual share of realized yield.

## Illustrative formula

`average eligible balance × realized annual yield × contracted business share`

For example, 1 billion USDC × 4% realized annual yield × 50% contracted share equals 20 million USDC in annual business revenue.

This is not a forecast or promise. The result changes with participation and active time. Rates, fees, losses, withdrawals, or commercial terms can change it again.

## Responsibility split

| Party | Owns |
| --- | --- |
| Customer | Account approval, reserve choice, risk acceptance, and exit decisions. |
| Business | Product interface, disclosures, support, compliance analysis, and customer relationship. |
| Loyal | Operation and reporting of Loyal's contracted automation service. |

The complete product structure determines legal and regulatory duties. Loyal's architecture does not create a blanket compliance conclusion.
