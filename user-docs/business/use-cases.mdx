title: "Neobank and payment use cases"
description: "How the same balance-sweep model appears in two financial products."
---

Both examples use the same automation. The customer chooses an operating reserve, eligible surplus moves into Earn, and the customer controls pause or exit. The business meaning changes below.

<Tabs>
  <Tab id="neobank" title="Neobank">
    ### Neobank

    | Decision | Answer |
    | --- | --- |
    | Reserve meaning | USDC needed for everyday spending. |
    | Business duty | Show available funds separately from the Earn position. |
    | Current limit | Loyal does not withdraw from Earn automatically before a future card payment. |
  </Tab>
  <Tab id="payment-processor" title="Payment processor">
    ### Payment processor

    | Decision | Answer |
    | --- | --- |
    | Reserve meaning | USDC needed for refunds and payouts. |
    | Business duty | Own payout forecasting, customer disclosures, and operational support. |
    | Current limit | Loyal does not withdraw from Earn automatically before a future payout. |
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

See [How Loyal works](/automations/how-it-works) for the common mechanism. Review [Product fit](/business/product-fit) before treating either example as an integration recommendation.
