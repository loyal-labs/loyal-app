---
title: "Integration readiness"
description: "The decisions a business must own before integrating Loyal."
icon: "clipboard-check"
---

Assign an owner and evidence for every decision before production access.

| Decision | Evidence required |
| --- | --- |
| Account model | Each customer has a distinct account with documented root authority. |
| Approval | The interface names the reserve and recurring access. It also names the destination and variable-yield risk. |
| Exit | Pause and withdrawal are distinct. Recurring close and full Earn exit are also distinct. |
| Legal and support | Product counsel records the custody and disclosure analysis for each jurisdiction. Support owns delayed sweeps, failed confirmations, and delayed withdrawals. |
| Environment and security | Engineering verifies configured program identities. Security maps deployed versions to available review evidence. |
| Operations and rollout | Operations owns monitoring and incident response. The rollout has a named limit, success measure, and stop condition. |

<Info>
  Readiness records who made each decision and the evidence they used. It does
  not decide whether an integration is legally noncustodial or risk-free.
</Info>

Developers can start with [Build with Loyal](/build/overview). Product owners should review [Ownership and control](/trust/ownership-and-control).
