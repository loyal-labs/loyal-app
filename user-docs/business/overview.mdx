Your customers may need some USDC ready for cards, refunds, or payouts. The rest can sit untouched because moving it requires manual operations or broader access than the customer wants to grant.

Loyal lets you offer an opt-in alternative. The customer chooses what stays available and approves a narrow path for eligible USDC to move into Earn. Loyal runs that workflow while the customer keeps root control of the account.

<Info>
  **Current product:** Balance Sweep & Earn. It moves eligible USDC after it
  reaches the customer's account. It does not predict a future payment and
  withdraw funds before that payment occurs.
</Info>

## What Loyal adds

  <Card title="A simple customer choice" icon="sliders">
