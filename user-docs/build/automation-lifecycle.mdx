Treat a transition as complete only when its durable verifier agrees.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Setup pending">
    Customer approval has started, but required onchain authority or account setup is not confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Active">
    Required authority exists and Loyal may evaluate eligible actions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paused">
    Loyal schedules no new sweeps. Onchain authority can remain.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Closing">
    The customer approved cleanup, but the required chain effects are not fully confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Closed">
    The close requirements are confirmed and durable coordination state records the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Failed">
    The intended transition did not complete. Current state must be refetched before retry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Source of truth by concern

| Concern | Source of truth |
| --- | --- |
| Root and policy authority | Current Solana accounts |
| Recurring authority | Current subscription and token accounts |
| Wallet and vault balances; lending position | Solana plus the underlying lending program |
| Operating floor and pause target | Loyal's durable orchestration state |
| Submitted or confirmed transaction | Signature status and affected onchain accounts |
| Reconciled history | Loyal record linked to confirmed chain evidence |

## Transition rule

Prepared work is not submitted work. Submission does not prove confirmation. Reconciliation links durable history to confirmed evidence, while a fresh onchain read proves current authority and balances. Persist only the transition supported by its required evidence.

Exact policy construction belongs in [Policy fields and construction](/earn/policies). Exit instructions belong in [Pause and close](/build/pause-and-close). Use [Runtime state and evidence](/build/state-events-and-receipts) to inspect a stall or failure.
