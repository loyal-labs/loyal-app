title: "Frequently asked questions"
description: "Short answers about Loyal automation, Earn, account control, and risk."
---

These answers summarize the product. Follow the linked page when you need the full explanation.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What can Loyal automate today?">
    The live workflow is USDC Balance Sweep and Earn. It keeps a chosen balance
    available, moves eligible surplus into Earn, and uses approved USDC lending
    actions. [See the workflow](/automations/how-it-works).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who controls the account?">
    For a default Earn account created by Loyal, the customer holds root Smart
    Account authority. Loyal uses a separate signer limited by the approved
    rule. [Review safety and control](/trust/ownership-and-control).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Loyal noncustodial?">
    The default Earn architecture does not require Loyal to hold or act as the
    customer's private key or root signer. Legal classification still depends on
    the integration and jurisdiction.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where does Earn yield come from?">
    Earn supplies USDC to approved Kamino lending markets. Borrower demand and
    market conditions produce a variable rate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is the APY fixed or guaranteed?">
    No. Rates can rise or fall, principal can be lost, and liquidity can delay a
    withdrawal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I stop an automation?">
    Pause stops new Loyal-scheduled work but does not revoke access. Withdrawal
    returns funds through the customer-approved path. A confirmed,
    customer-signed close removes Autodeposit authority.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Has Loyal been audited?">
    Squads Smart Accounts, Kamino Lending, and Solana Foundation Subscriptions
    publish security reviews. Those reports are version or commit scoped.
    Loyal's complete application and integration have not received a standalone
    external audit. [See the reports and remaining
    risk](/trust/ownership-and-control).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What could Loyal automate next?">
    The model can support more workflows when their trigger, action, limits,
    proof, and exit are bounded. Subscriptions, settlement routing, and treasury
    allocation are illustrative, not available products or roadmap commitments.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
