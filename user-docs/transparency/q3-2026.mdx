### Spending Analysis

- **Revenue (2,448.97)** is the first revenue Loyal has reported. All of it comes from Loyal's own capital working in Loyal products, and we count it all as Autonomous Vault revenue.
- **Total spend fell about 10% from Q2** (155,159.20 vs 172,839.34), driven by lower payroll.

- **Equipment (0.35%):** a 537.00 reimbursement for DDR5 RAM in September.

## Governance Actions

**LOYAL-003** passed on Jul 21, 2026 and went live on Jul 30:

- **Autonomous Vault.** Part of the DAO's capital now runs inside the Loyal Autonomous Vault. Only the DAO's Squads treasury multisig can sign for it. Under its policy the vault cannot send funds to an outside address, cannot sell LOYAL, cannot mint or burn, and cannot change its own policy.
- **Team allocation.** The unvested team allocation was burned and replaced with milestone-based minting.
- **Proposal stake.** The stake needed to put up a proposal rose from 500,000 to 1,500,000 LOYAL.
- **OTC window.** Up to 2,142,857 LOYAL can be sold OTC, at the higher of a 7-day TWAP or a $0.14 floor, with proceeds going to the treasury.

Details are on [metadao.fi](https://www.metadao.fi/decisions/LOYAL-003).

Loyal's treasury is governed through MetaDAO proposals and executed transparently on-chain. Operational reporting follows a cash basis. All figures are denominated in USDC. Questions: [main@askloyal.com](mailto:main@askloyal.com).
