The default Loyal Earn setup separates customer root authority from Loyal's limited automation signer.

<AuthorityMap />

Loyal does not receive or hold the customer's private key. It cannot grant itself root authority or change approved rules.

Externally created Smart Accounts can use different settings from Loyal's default Earn setup. The architecture can support a customer-controlled, noncustodial design; legal classification depends on the product and jurisdiction.

Pause stops new Loyal-scheduled sweeps but can leave approved access in place. A confirmed customer-approved close removes recurring sweep access. Withdrawal and full Earn exit are separate actions. See [Exit and lifecycle](/automations/exit-and-lifecycle).
