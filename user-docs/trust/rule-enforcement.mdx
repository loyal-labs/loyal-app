---

Loyal's signer can submit an automation transaction. The transaction still has to satisfy the recurring delegation and the Smart Account policy.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Allowed">
    The action follows the approved path and stays within its limit. The onchain checks pass, so the transaction can proceed.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Rejected">
    The action leaves the approved path or exceeds its limit. The onchain check fails before that off-policy action executes.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Two separate boundaries

The recurring delegation limits when and how much Loyal may move. The Smart Account policy separately limits the allowed route. Both checks must pass.

These controls limit Loyal's signer. They do not protect against every defect in an allowed program or lending market.

Developers can inspect the exact [policy fields and construction](/earn/policies). Customers can review [Ownership and control](/trust/ownership-and-control).
