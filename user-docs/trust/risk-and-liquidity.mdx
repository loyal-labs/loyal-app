---
title: "Risk and liquidity"
description: "The risks that remain after Loyal limits automation authority."
---

A narrow automation boundary reduces what Loyal can execute. It does not remove protocol, asset, liquidity, network, or operational risk.

| Risk | Customer-visible consequence |
| --- | --- |
| Smart Account or policy defect | An intended action can fail, or an allowed boundary can behave incorrectly. |
| Subscription defect | A recurring sweep can fail or its delegated behavior can differ from the intended schedule. |
| Lending-market loss | Principal can fall. |
| USDC depeg | The dollar value of USDC can fall. |
| Low market liquidity | A withdrawal can be delayed or receive less favorable execution. |
| Compromised Loyal signer | The signer can submit any transaction that still fits the approved boundary. |
| Solana or RPC outage | Submission, confirmation, or current-state reads can be delayed. |
| Loyal service outage | Scheduling or reporting can be delayed without changing customer root authority. |

<Warning>
  Earn rates vary. Principal is not insured, and access can depend on lending-market liquidity.
</Warning>
