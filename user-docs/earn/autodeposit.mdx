---
title: "Autodeposit subscription"
description: "How to create and observe the recurring sweep subscription, plus its update boundary."
---

Autodeposit is for users who want Earn to collect eligible USDC automatically while using a chosen Main Account amount as the operating target.

Loyal treats the floor as a scheduling target. It calculates surplus above that target and checks separate onchain limits before execution.

## Key Facts

| Question                     | Short answer                                                  |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| What does the user choose?   | A wallet floor plus the recurring period and amount           |
| What does Loyal watch?       | The wallet's USDC token account                               |
| What can Loyal schedule?     | Calculated surplus, further limited by the approved delegation |
| Where does it go first?      | The Earn vault                                                |
| What happens after the pull? | The executor can top up the active Kamino Earn route          |

## Setup

Autodeposit setup creates the onchain pieces needed for a constrained pull:

- A subscription authority for your wallet and USDC mint
- A recurring delegation with its schedule and amount for each period
- An Autodeposit policy fixed to the expected USDC path and Earn vault
- A saved floor that Loyal uses when calculating eligible surplus

You approve this setup from your Main Account. Loyal does not become a root Smart Account signer or receive an unrestricted destination.

<Info>
  The saved floor is an automation setting used by Loyal's scheduler. The
  recurring delegation defines the period and allowed amount. The Smart
  Account policy separately constrains the USDC mint, source and destination
  accounts, subscription program, instruction shape, and maximum amount per
  period.
</Info>

## How Wallet Watching Works

Loyal uses Solana account subscriptions to notice changes to watched USDC token accounts. When your wallet balance rises above the floor, the system can schedule the eligible surplus for a sweep, usually with a delay before execution.

That schedule gives the executor time to verify the current wallet balance, the policy/delegation state, and the Earn route before moving funds.

## Execution

<Steps>
  <Step title="Detect surplus">
    The balance watcher sees that the wallet has USDC above the saved floor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Schedule a sweep">
    Loyal records the eligible amount and waits until it is ready to execute.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pull to Earn">
    The executor uses the Autodeposit policy to move the scheduled USDC through
    the fixed path from the wallet token account into the Earn vault.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up Earn">
    After the pull, the executor can deposit the new vault USDC into the current
    Kamino Earn route.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Update boundary

Updating the floor changes Loyal's eligibility calculation. It does not rewrite the subscription period or Smart Account policy. Pausing and closing change different state; use [Pause and close](/build/pause-and-close) for their transaction and confirmation requirements.

<Warning>
  Autodeposit is worker-driven. Execution can take time because the worker
  verifies state before moving funds.
</Warning>
